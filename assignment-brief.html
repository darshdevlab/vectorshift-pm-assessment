VectorShift Assignment Brief

Product Manager (Platform)

Open original document

Overview

This assessment is about evaluating a real product: exploring the VectorShift builder, forming a view on what should be improved, and making a clear case for the recommended changes.

Access

Accept the VectorShift invite and access the platform at app.vectorshift.ai/projects.

The Task

  1. Explore the builder view: Projects, Knowledge Bases, Tables, Skills, Marketplace, and Analytics.
  2. Form a view on who VectorShift is for and the top use cases it solves.
  3. Build one selected use case as a working workflow or agent, end to end.
  4. Identify and rank the top things to fix or improve in the builder view.
  5. Deep dive on the #1 issue, including how to fix it with engineering, how to measure success, and a prototype.
  6. Create a rough prototype for the #1 solution: mockup, sketch, Figma frame, or AI-built prototype.
  7. Submit a short 2-3 page document with the built use case, ranked issues, and #1 issue solution.
  8. Bonus: add a brief competitor analysis covering 1-2 relevant platforms.

Deliverable

  1. Who VectorShift is for and its top use cases.
  2. Link or screenshots of the use case built.
  3. Ranked list of issues, aiming for five high-quality findings.
  4. Deep dive on the #1 issue with a prototype of the solution.
  5. Optional bonus: brief competitor analysis of one or two platforms.
